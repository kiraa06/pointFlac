OPRA headphone EQ database
--------------------------

Headphone correction presets (manufacturer, product and EQ data) and the OPRA logo come from the OPRA project,
https://github.com/opra-project/OPRA, created and maintained by Roon Labs and the OPRA community.
Built from commit 0b88ecd4e2bef7cf69fd5d50f1d06fb586c10865.

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en
Each preset is credited to its author in the app ("EQ by <author> · OPRA"). The data was converted to a compact
JSON form and presets the equaliser cannot represent were left out; the data is otherwise unmodified. The
conversion script is scripts/build-opra.swift. Only the data is under CC BY-SA 4.0; pointFlac's own code is not.
